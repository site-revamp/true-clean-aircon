# TrueClean Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

TrueClean Aircon provides air-conditioning cleaning, chemical treatment, and maintenance in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps customers compare cleaning options, understand what a service involves, and request an assessment.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make cleaning options easier to compare.** Give general servicing, chemical wash, and chemical overhaul distinct service summaries. Explain what each covers and when a technician might recommend it, so customers can discuss the right option for their unit.
2. **Explain what customers can expect from treatment.** Describe how the team assesses the unit, what the selected cleaning service involves, and what customers can expect during the visit. Include treatment claims and warranty terms only when supported.
3. **Connect common aircon problems to enquiries.** Give visitors a direct route from symptoms such as weak cooling, leaks, odours, and restricted airflow to an assessment request. Make Call and WhatsApp actions prominent on mobile using reconfirmed contact details.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service cards would place scope and suitability in the same position for each option. A dedicated cleaning-process section and clear enquiry buttons beside the service information would make the page easier to scan and act on.
